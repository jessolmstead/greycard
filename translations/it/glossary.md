# Italian (it) glossary for greycard's window

This is the glossary of a draft translation that no native speaker has
read yet. Corrections to it and to the catalog are welcome.

Sources: the Italian Lightroom Classic / Camera Raw wording first, Capture One
and darktable where Lightroom has no term or where noted. "Alt" is the other
editors' term when it differs.

## Conventions

- **Commands** (buttons, menu items): the imperative, which in Italian
  software is the bare verb form: Esporta, Ritaglia, Salva, Annulla,
  Elimina. Held everywhere; never the infinitive ("Esportare") on a control.
- **Sentences addressing the user**: impersonal where possible ("Ha effetto
  al prossimo avvio"); where a person must be addressed, the informal "tu"
  (Apple's and Microsoft's current Italian), never "Lei".
- **Capitalization**: sentence case (Italian never title-cases). Source ALL
  CAPS section headings stay ALL CAPS.
- **Keys**: Ctrl, Maiusc (Shift), Alt, Invio (Enter), Esc, Spazio (Space),
  Canc (Delete), Tab, Backspace, Home, Fine (End). Key letters unchanged.
  Key names inside the keys sheet's key column are data and stay.
- **Frame** (one photo): "foto", invariable, feminine ("{n} foto", "foto
  selezionata"). Short, and what Lightroom IT says.
- **Loanwords kept**: raw, file, preset, look, JPEG, TIFF, DNG, flash, zoom,
  pixel, sidecar, backup, cache, texture.
- Ellipsis, colons, and edge spaces exactly as the source has them.
  Numbers and dates stay in the English form (§616).
