# Traditional Chinese (zh_TW) glossary for greycard's window

This is the glossary of a draft translation that no native speaker has
read yet. Corrections to it and to the catalog are welcome.

Source of the terms: the Traditional Chinese Lightroom Classic (LrC)
and Photoshop first, the camera makers' Taiwanese menus (Canon, Sony,
Nikon, Fujifilm) where Adobe has none, and the words Taiwanese
photographers and Taiwan's desktop software use where neither has
one. It is written for Taiwan; Hong Kong and Macau users get it too,
as the nearest catalog, and a reader there may find words to change.

It is not the Simplified catalog in other characters. The column
“zh_CN” gives the Simplified catalog's word where Taiwan's differs,
and a blank where the two agree.

## Conventions

- **Address.** Buttons, menu items, and commands: a short verb or
  verb-object phrase, as Taiwan's desktop software writes them
  (匯出..., 開啟資料夾..., 全部重設). A question in a sheet's title:
  the plain question (「刪除 3 張相片？」). Status lines: impersonal,
  a done action with 已 (「已匯出 3 張相片」), one under way with 正在
  (「正在匯出 3 張相片...」). A sentence that must address the user is
  written impersonally; 您 would be the choice if one ever must, and
  no line needs it yet.
- **Punctuation.** Full-width in sentences: ，。：；？！ and 「」 for
  quotations (『』 inside them), as Taiwan writes them; （） around text
  that holds Chinese, half-width () around text that is only Latin,
  digits, or keys ((F11), (?), (--no-sidecars)). Enumerations of nouns
  take 、 and 和 or 或 before the last. Menu ellipses stay as the
  source has them (“...”), with no space before.
- **Spaces.** One space between Chinese and Latin letters, digits, or
  a placeholder that holds them ({n}, {name}, {file}, {label}): 「3 張
  相片」, 「匯出到 {folder}」. None next to full-width punctuation, none
  between a number and %, and none around a placeholder filled with
  Chinese words ({what}, {why}, {how}, {place}, {words}, {done}). This
  follows the Simplified catalog; Taiwan's software is not of one mind
  about it.
- **Tails.** An English tail joined on with “, ” or “; ” starts with
  ， or ； and no space. A list's middle item takes 、.
- **Headings.** The develop panel's ALL CAPS headings are plain Chinese
  (WHITE BALANCE → 白平衡).
- **Product words** stay as they are: greycard, Lightroom, RawTherapee,
  darktable, lensfun, GitHub, ONNX Runtime (the word Runtime alone,
  too), JPEG, TIFF, PNG, DNG, ICC, EXIF, XMP, RGB, HDR, GPU, CPU, file
  names, units (MB, KB, GiB, px, EV, K, mired, s), and the search's
  tokens (`camera:`, `iso>=`).
- **Keys.** Key names stay as the keys sheet draws them (Ctrl, Shift,
  Alt, Space, Enter, Esc, Tab). The pointer's words are Taiwan's
  Windows wording: 按一下 (click), 按兩下 (double-click), 按右鍵
  (right-click), 拖曳 (drag); “Ctrl+按一下”.
- **Numbers and dates** keep the English form (§616).
- **Plurals.** nplurals=1; plural=0: one form, the count before a
  measure word.
- **Measure words.** 張 for frames, pictures, and thumbnails (3 張
  相片); 個 for files, sidecars, folders, presets, looks, models, masks,
  and shapes; 組 for an export queue's sets; 項 for edits and abstract
  items; 次 for repeated actions (3 次復原).
- **Frame / picture.** A frame is 相片, as LrC and Canon write it
  (照片 is as common in speech); the picture, what is drawn, is 影像
  (Taiwan's word; 图像 is the mainland's).
- **Rows and columns.** Taiwan's 列 is a row and 欄 a column, the
  reverse of the mainland's 行 and 列; a bar across the window is 列
  (底片列, 篩選列).

## Develop

**編修**, from LrC's module name 編修相片. The full name would read
相片 twice in phrases such as 在編修相片中開啟相片, and 編修 alone is
what Taiwanese users call retouching and adjusting a photo; it is two
characters, narrower than the English, so the Loupe's mode row keeps
its width. The verb “develop” (a raw rendered for the screen or an
export) is 處理: 「正在處理...」, 「已處理」. The noun “edit” (what an
edit holds) stays 編輯, as the Simplified catalog has it.

## Choices most in doubt

- 編修 for Develop against the full 編修相片.
- 匯出 for Export against LrC's 轉存.
- 自然飽和度 for Vibrance against LrC's 細節飽和度.
- 雜訊 for Noise against LrC's 雜色.
- 挑片 for Cull against 選片.
- 色標 for Label against LrC's 色彩標籤.
- 底片列 for Filmstrip.
