# Spanish (es) glossary for greycard's window

This is the glossary of a draft translation that no native speaker has
read yet. Corrections to it and to the catalog are welcome.

Neutral international Spanish, readable in Spain and Latin America.
Source of terms: the Spanish Lightroom Classic (LR), Capture One (C1),
and darktable (dt); where they differ, LR's wins and the other is
noted.

## Conventions

- **Commands and buttons: infinitive.** "Exportar", "Recortar",
  "Guardar como...", "Deshacer". Menu items that name a thing stay nouns.
- **Address: impersonal or "usted".** Never "vosotros", never "tú".
  Prefer impersonal phrasing ("No hay...", "Se moverán...") and the
  infinitive; where a person must be addressed, "usted" forms
  ("Elija", "Pulse", "Vuelva a intentarlo").
- **Capitalization: sentence case** (Spanish convention), first word
  only, plus proper names. Source ALL CAPS section headings stay ALL
  CAPS ("LUZ", "COLOR", "RECORTE").
- **Status-line fragments** the source writes in lower case stay lower
  case; leading ", " "; " " " and trailing punctuation kept exactly.
- **Ellipsis:** as the source has it ("..." stays three dots, "…"
  stays the glyph).
- **Inverted marks:** "¿...?" and "¡...!" wherever the source has a
  question or exclamation.
- **Colon:** no space before it (unlike French): "Prueba: {}".
- **Keys in prose:** Ctrl, Alt, Mayús (Shift), Supr (Delete), Retroceso
  (Backspace), Intro (Enter), Esc, Tab, Inicio, Fin, flechas. A chord
  built from the keys table is data and is not in the catalog. Where a
  key name sits inside a string that may be compared, it stays English.
- **Numbers and units** unchanged (EV, px, MB, %, K).
- **Gender:** a frame is "foto" (feminine): "rechazada", "elegida",
  "importada(s)", "la foto abierta".
- **Plurals:** nplurals=2; plural=(n != 1). Both forms filled.
- **Neutral vocabulary:** "archivo" (not "fichero"), "pulsar/hacer
  clic" (not "picar/cliquear"), "computadora/ordenador" avoided (say
  "equipo" or rephrase), "elegir" (not "escoger"), "borrar/eliminar"
  (eliminar for delete), "papelera" (both regions, the OS's word).

## The debatable ones

1. Highlights "Luces" vs LR's "Iluminaciones" (clips) or "Altas luces" (C1, dt).
2. Preset "Preajuste" vs LR's "Ajuste preestablecido".
3. Look "Aspecto" vs "Estilo" (C1) or "Perfil creativo" (LR).
4. Cull "Criba" vs "Descarte" or "Selección".
5. Settings "Preferencias" (LR) vs "Configuración".
6. Sidecar "archivo auxiliar" vs "archivo XMP" (LR) or "sidecar".
7. Dehaze "Neblina" vs LR's "Quitar neblina".
8. Defringe "Desflecar" (dt) vs "Eliminar halos" (LR).
9. Tint "Matiz" (LR) vs "Tinte".
10. Frame "foto" vs "fotograma" or "imagen".
11. Heal "Corregir" (LR) vs "Reparar".
12. Luma/Chroma "Luma/Croma" vs LR's "Luminancia/Color".
13. Watermark "Filigrana" vs LR's "Marca de agua" (clips).
14. Lens "Lente" vs "Objetivo".
15. Cull "Criba" also names the tab; LR has no such module.
