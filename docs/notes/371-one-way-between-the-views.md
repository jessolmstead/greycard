# §371. One way between the views (2026-10-18)

The grid's header kept a Loupe button at its right end, beside
Rejects and Lightbox, from before the two views: it was the way out
of the grid when the header was the only place for one. The switch
at the top of the left pane (§359) then arrived on top of it, and
the window had two buttons doing one thing, with Enter and a
double-click on a tile doing it too. The header's button goes.
Rejects and Lightbox stay there, since they are things the grid
offers rather than places to go, and the pane's switch is the one way
between Library and Loupe.
