# §427. Shift and the wheel on a Mac

*2026-10-25*

The wheel moves any slider with Shift held. On a Mac, Shift held
turns a vertical scroll into a horizontal one, leftward for up, and
the slider read the sideways delta with its own sign, so a swipe up
moved the value down. A sideways delta under Shift is now read as
the vertical it was; a real sideways scroll without Shift keeps
right as up. One test holds Shift and scrolls both ways.
