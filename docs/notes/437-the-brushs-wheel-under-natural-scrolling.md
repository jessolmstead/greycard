# §437. The brush's wheel under natural scrolling

*2026-10-26*

With the sliders turned back under natural scrolling (§432), the brush
and the retouch patch still sized the system's way: a two-finger swipe
up on a Mac shrank them. The wheel over the viewport grows them on a
positive delta (§303), a wheel rolled down, which natural scrolling
sends for a swipe down.

A size is a value, not content, the distinction §432 drew for a slider,
so the viewport turns the delta back the same way, from the sliders'
global, before it is added up: the brush and the patch grow with a swipe
up. The picture's zoom and pan keep the system's way, as lists do,
since there content following the fingers is right.
