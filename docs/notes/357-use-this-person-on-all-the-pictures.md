# §357. Use this person on all the pictures (2026-10-18)

A People mask of one person (§263) pasted or synced onto a set
arrives asking on every frame: the person is stored as a signature
and a place, and each frame must find her again, with a click where
the match cannot decide alone. On a group sequence that was a click
per frame, which was the item §263 left open.

## The offer

After a paste or sync that carried a People mask of a person onto
several frames, a click on the person for that shape, on any of the
frames it went to, offers "Use this person on all N pictures" under
the shapes, beside a cross that puts it away. N is this frame and
the others that still hold the shape as it came. All people never
offers, nor does the frame the sync came from, nor a set of one. The
offer belongs to that paste or sync: the cross or a run ends it, a
later paste or sync of People masks replaces it, and one with no
People mask leaves it. It is dropped on any history move of the
frame it was picked on, and hidden whenever that frame's shape no
longer holds the person picked, checked again at the press.

## The run

Each other frame goes to the worker as a job of its own, decoded
and developed reduced as the masks of frames not open are made,
with the editor's People model lent to it rather than a second copy
loaded. The jobs queue after the open frame's work and after the
exports, since exports were asked first and a person a run has not
reached is still one click away. Between jobs the model stays in
the editor's copy, so a frame opened mid-run with a People mask
shares it, and the last job releases it unless the open frame is
picking or holds a live People shape. A panic in a job drops the
lent model and answers that frame, and touches nothing of the open
frame's state.

A frame takes the person by the match §263 already has, with one
more rule. A pick made on a portrait decides by the signature
alone. A pick made on a group, where two or more faces were offered,
takes the match only when it is also the face at the place the
person was clicked on the first frame, in that frame's units, and
only when the two frames share a turn and an aspect; colors alone
never decide on a group, and the place alone is whoever stands
there. The write-time check is the whole shape against the frame's
current edit, not the edit at the press, so a frame picked by hand
or edited back since is left alone and its other edits stand.
Each frame written gets one history step of its own, "Person from"
the frame clicked, in its own sidecar, undone there. The open frame,
when its own answer lands, takes it through the panel as a click
would. The status line counts the outcomes: used, kept its own
person, not found, changed since, could not be looked at.

Nothing new goes into a sidecar: the signature, the place and the
hash of the frame it was made on are what a Person stored already.

## Left

The offer covers the one shape clicked, not another People shape of
the same person on the same frames; a pick of All people offers
nothing; a newer run does not cancel an older run's queued jobs,
whose answers are ignored. A slider moved and undone on the picked
frame drops the offer, since any history move does. The open frame's
own answer merges with a panel change still inside the save delay,
under the panel's label rather than "Person from". The cross is not
reachable from the keyboard, as the shapes' Remove cross is not. The
rule has not been run against a real group sequence with the model;
the tests deliver the worker's answers, as the People tests do.

## Tests

The rule on real signatures: found by colors and place, the lone
face on a group pick refused without the place, the place unknown
across a turn or aspect, a portrait pick by colors alone. The window:
a pick after a sync onto three offered for all and used; a paste
offers it too; frames she is not found on left waiting and counted,
with the already-decided frame, the open frame and a stale run's
answer; no offer for one frame or for All people; the offer put
away, gone with its frame, gone when the pick is undone or the shape
changes; each frame undoes its own person; a frame changed since the
press left alone, its sidecar byte for byte; the open frame's answer
putting its ask down and saved after the timer.
